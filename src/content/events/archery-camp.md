**Event Timing:** July 22–25, 2024

**Elementary Camp** (incoming 3rd–6th grade): 9:00–10:00am, 10:00–11:00am, or 11:00am–12:00pm

**JH/SH Camp** (incoming 7th–12th grade): 1:00–2:30pm

**Event Location:** Heart Shot Ministry, building in the parking lot of Cornerstone Baptist Church, [775 E LeClaire Rd., Eldridge, IA 52748](https://www.google.com/maps/search/775+E+LeClaire+Rd.,+Eldridge,+IA+52748)

Contact Troy Bendickson at [troy@heartshot.org](mailto:troy@heartshot.org) or 678-492-1989.

**Cost:** $35 for Elementary; $50 for JH/HS

The Elementary Camp is open to anyone, not just the North Scott Community — each hour is capped at 20 campers. The JH/HS Camp is also open to anyone, and will have many Lancer Archery Team members attending, capped at 30 campers.

Registration includes everything needed, from instruction to equipment. Camp t-shirt included if registration and payment are completed by July 1st. Registration deadline: Monday, July 15th.
