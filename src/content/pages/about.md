### Vision

All patrons fall in love with archery and Jesus Christ, or grow an already existing love of both.

### Mission

We share Jesus Christ through the fundamentals of archery and serve widows in our communities. We teach about Jesus through archery inside our ranges, and ask our patrons to join us in being like Jesus outside of our ranges through service.

### Values

Rooted in God's Word, Guided by the Holy Spirit, Yoked to Jesus Christ, Covered Prayerfully, Pursued Dynamically, Fueled Relationally, Serving Proactively.

## The Heart Shot Ministry Model

We are open 5 days a week between 2 facilities, free to the public. We provide all the equipment needed, also free of charge, but archers can also bring their own equipment. Our sessions include archery instruction and practice, lessons from scripture and prayer, and a shooting incentive program.

We host donation-based private groups and travel with our mobile program to churches, retreats, and camps.

Word of mouth has grown the ministry most effectively, but we also have a web and social media presence.

The archery ranges have served as a gathering place for community, creating a network of resources that moves us toward a comprehensive and proactive approach to widow outreach.

Our distinction is that we invite families and individuals of all ages to experience archery in a safe and faith-filled environment without financial obligation or commitment.

## Funding Plan

- 100% of funds raised through private donors, private groups, and sale of concessions.
- Board members and volunteer instructors receive no compensation.
- The CEO's family is supported by private donors, who give through Heart Shot Ministry Inc.
- New funding comes through outdoor programs, sponsorship of the new property, sponsorship of the outdoor range, sponsorship of widow care, and fundraising events.

## Results to Date

- Attendance to date for all Heart Shot Ministry programs has been slightly over 22,000.
- Hosted or traveled to hundreds of private groups, including youth groups, adult small groups, birthday parties, cub/boy scouts, staff meetings, camp sessions, retreats, schools, and more.
- Serving many widows and single ladies in our community with snow removal, lawn care, and miscellaneous repairs and upkeep as requested.
